Privacy policy

Last updated 14 November 2026

What we store

  • Account: your email address, a salted hash of a value derived from your password (never the password), your wrapped (encrypted) vault key and, for Google sign-in, your Google account identifier and name.
  • Devices: for each signed-in device, its name, platform, when it was created and last used.
  • Clipboard: encrypted items only. We cannot read them. Items are deleted after 7 days or when you delete them, and at most the latest 100 are kept.

What we do not do

  • We do not read, analyse, sell or share your clipboard or account data.
  • We use no advertising or analytics trackers and no third-party scripts.

Who processes data for us

Email delivery (confirmation and password-reset messages) is handled by an email provider, and Google handles authentication if you choose Google sign-in. Only the data needed for those messages or sign-ins is sent.

Cookies

One strictly necessary session cookie (uc_session) keeps you signed in on the web app. There are no tracking cookies.

Your choices

You can sign out any device, delete all clipboard items, or delete your account (Settings → Delete account) at any time; deleting removes your account, sessions and clipboard data from the server.
