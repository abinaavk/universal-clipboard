uclip: how it works

Accounts, devices, and how a copy travels. Clipboard text is encrypted on your device; the relay only ever holds ciphertext.
1

Website · uclip.gaiasentinel.online

Create your account with email + password or Google. Your password never leaves the browser: it unlocks a random vault key that stays encrypted on the server.

⇄

Relay (server)

Stores accounts, device sessions and ciphertext only. Pushes new clips live to every active session over a streaming connection. Cannot read anything.

⇄
3

Active sessions only

Each device has its own session. Sign out or revoke it on the website and that device stops sending and receiving immediately and wipes its key.

① CopyUse the normal copy command on any signed-in device. The daemon (or the open tab) notices it.
② EncryptAES-256-GCM with your vault key, on the device, before anything is uploaded.
③ RelayThe server stores the ciphertext and streams it to your other active sessions in about a second.
④ PasteEach device decrypts and puts it on its own clipboard. Paste anywhere. Different accounts never see each other's clips.
